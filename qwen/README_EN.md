# ChatCut Video Editor

Create talking-head videos, motion graphics and product promos in ChatCut, with AI media generation and an editable multitrack timeline.

For creators, content editors and product marketing teams. Start with a motion-graphics explainer, a product promo or a talking-head edit. Align on the brief, prepare media, build the editable timeline and review the result. Export when requested.

Sign in through the ChatCut connector in QwenWork. No ChatCut Desktop installation is required. Selected local media is uploaded to the ChatCut cloud; generation may consume ChatCut credits. If the connector is unavailable, work can continue on scripts or editing plans without claiming that a cloud project has been changed.
